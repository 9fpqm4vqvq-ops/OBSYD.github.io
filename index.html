Our Story

OBSYD started from passion and curiosity. The founder began learning HTML and web creation from scratch, driven by a strong interest in technology, design, and creativity. This inspired the creation of OBSYD.

Our Vision

While exploring websites in the local market, it became clear that many companies lacked strong design, modern interfaces, and unique branding online. We are here to change that narrative.

What We Believe

OBSYD focuses on:

  • Modern website creation
  • Creative branding
  • Custom digital experiences
  • Strong visual identity

Our Mission

The goal of the agency is to create clean, modern, and visually unique websites that reflect the identity of each company instead of generic templates.
